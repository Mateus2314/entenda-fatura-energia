# 06 - Target Audience

## 1. Primary User Types

### 1.1 Client (End Consumer)

#### Demographics
- **Age Range:** 25-65 years old
- **Location:** Brazil (all states, focus on urban areas initially)
- **Education Level:** High school to university degree
- **Income Level:** Middle class and above (R$ 3,000+ monthly income)
- **Housing:** Homeowners or long-term renters
- **Tech Literacy:** Basic to intermediate (comfortable using web apps)

#### Characteristics
- Receives monthly electricity bills
- Confused by complex tariff structures
- Wants to reduce energy costs
- Concerned about environmental impact
- Limited time to research energy optimization
- May not have technical background in engineering/energy

#### Pain Points
- Cannot understand breakdown of charges on bill
- Suspects overcharging but cannot verify
- Doesn't know how to reduce consumption effectively
- Confused by tariff flags (yellow, red levels)
- Uncertain which tariff modality is best
- No clear action plan for energy savings
- Difficulty comparing bills month-to-month

#### Goals
- Understand exactly what they're paying for
- Validate bill accuracy against ANEEL tariffs
- Identify specific ways to save money
- Make informed decisions about energy usage
- Track consumption trends over time
- Reduce monthly electricity expenses
- Contribute to environmental sustainability

#### Usage Patterns
- Logs in monthly (when bill arrives)
- Spends 10-20 minutes per session
- Primarily interested in own consumption
- Downloads reports for personal records
- May share findings with family
- Uses on desktop and mobile

#### Technical Comfort
- Can fill out online forms
- Comfortable with email and basic web navigation
- May need guidance on complex features
- Prefers visual representations (charts) over raw data
- Appreciates tooltips and help text

### 1.2 Consultant / Energy Efficiency Professional

#### Demographics
- **Age Range:** 28-55 years old
- **Location:** Brazil, major cities
- **Education Level:** University degree or technical certification
- **Specialization:** Electrical engineering, energy efficiency, sustainability
- **Employment:** Independent consultant or small consulting firm
- **Experience:** 2+ years in energy sector
- **Tech Literacy:** Intermediate to advanced

#### Characteristics
- Manages multiple clients (5-50 accounts)
- Provides energy auditing and consulting services
- Needs professional reporting capabilities
- Values efficiency and scalability
- Has technical knowledge of electricity systems
- May serve residential or commercial clients
- Seeks competitive advantage through technology

#### Pain Points
- Time-consuming manual bill analysis
- Difficult to manage multiple client accounts
- Inconsistent report quality
- Hard to track client portfolio performance
- Limited resources for software development
- Need to maintain professional brand image
- Clients expect detailed, understandable reports
- Difficulty demonstrating value/ROI to clients

#### Goals
- Streamline bill analysis workflow
- Manage multiple clients efficiently
- Generate professional branded reports quickly
- Track all clients' savings in one place
- Demonstrate expertise and value to clients
- Scale consulting business without hiring more staff
- Provide consistent, high-quality service
- Differentiate from competitors

#### Usage Patterns
- Logs in multiple times per week
- Analyzes 10-50 bills per month
- Spends 1-3 hours per session
- Bulk operations preferred
- Needs client management features
- Frequently generates and shares reports
- Uses primarily on desktop

#### Technical Comfort
- Comfortable with complex software
- Can troubleshoot minor issues
- Appreciates keyboard shortcuts and efficiency features
- May customize settings and preferences
- Willing to learn advanced features
- Values integration possibilities

#### Willingness to Pay
- **High** - sees platform as business tool
- Expected to be primary revenue source in future
- Would pay for premium features:
  - White-label reports
  - Advanced analytics
  - Priority support
  - Bulk operations
  - API access

### 1.3 Energy Efficiency Company

#### Demographics
- **Company Size:** Small to medium (5-100 employees)
- **Location:** Brazil, state capitals

- **Years in Business:** 3+ years

- Needs scalability and team collaboration

- Inconsistent analysis quality across staff
- Time-intensive manual processes
- Difficulty tracking company-wide metrics
- Client onboarding inefficiency
- Reporting bottlenecks
- No centralized client database

#### Goals

- Improve operational efficiency
- Scale client acquisition
- Reduce time-per-analysis
- Enhance service quality
- Centralize client data
- Track company-wide KPIs
- Improve client retention
- Generate recurring revenue

#### Usage Patterns
- Multiple users logging in daily
- High volume of bills processed (100s per month)
- Team collaboration needed
- Requires role-based access
- Needs admin oversight
- Exports data for external systems
- Integration with CRM desired (future)

#### Technical Comfort
- Has IT support available
- Can handle complex enterprise software
- May need onboarding and training
- Values documentation and support
- Interested in API integration (future)

#### Willingness to Pay
- **Very High** - critical business tool
- Expected to be key revenue source
- Enterprise tier pricing acceptable
- Would pay for:
  - Multi-user accounts
  - Advanced permissions
  - White-label solution
  - Dedicated support

  - Custom integrations

## 2. Secondary Audiences

### 2.1 Environmentally Conscious Consumers
- Primary motivation: reduce carbon footprint
- Interested in environmental impact metrics
- Willing to invest in green solutions
- Values sustainability reporting
- Often overlaps with Client persona

### 2.2 Property Managers
- Manage multiple properties/units
- Need to optimize costs across portfolio
- May analyze many bills for different locations
- Similar to Consultant but focused on own properties
- Future feature: bulk property management

### 2.3 Condominiums / HOAs
- Manage shared energy costs
- Need to break down charges fairly
- Present findings to residents
- Require transparency and documentation
- Future feature: multi-unit buildings

### 2.4 Small Business Owners
- Have commercial electricity accounts
- Higher consumption than residential
- More complex tariff structures (Group A)
- Interested in operational cost reduction
- Future feature: commercial account support

### 2.5 Educational Institutions
- Use for teaching energy efficiency concepts
- May analyze their own facilities
- Students learning about sustainability
- Need educational resources and examples
- Future feature: educational mode

## 3. User Personas

### Persona 1: Maria - The Concerned Homeowner

**Quote:** "I just want to know if I'm being charged correctly and how I can save money."

**Background:**
- 38 years old, accountant, lives in São Paulo
- Homeowner with family (2 adults, 2 children)
- Electricity bill: R$ 350-450/month
- Recently noticed bill increasing without explanation

**Behavior:**
- Checks bill when it arrives, once per month
- Compares current bill with previous months
- Searches online for energy-saving tips
- Skeptical of bill accuracy but doesn't know how to verify

**Needs:**
- Simple, clear explanation of charges
- Validation that charges are correct
- Practical tips she can implement immediately
- Visual comparison of consumption over time

**Success Metrics:**
- Understands bill within 5 minutes
- Identifies 2-3 actionable savings opportunities
- Reduces monthly bill by 10-15%

## 4. Market Segmentation

### 4.1 Geographic Segmentation

**Primary Market (MVP):**
- **São Paulo State** - Largest population, highest energy consumption
- **Rio de Janeiro State** - Second largest market
- **Minas Gerais** - Third largest market

**Secondary Market (Phase 2):**
- **Other Southeast states** (Espírito Santo)
- **South Region** (Paraná, Santa Catarina, Rio Grande do Sul)
- **Central-West** (Distrito Federal, Goiás, Mato Grosso do Sul)

**Tertiary Market (Phase 3):**
- **Northeast Region** - Different tariff structures, growing market
- **North Region** - Unique energy challenges

**International (Phase 4+):**
- Latin America (similar systems)
- Other markets based on demand

### 4.2 Demographic Segmentation

**By Income:**
- **Primary:** Middle to upper-middle class (R$ 3k-15k monthly income)
- **Secondary:** Upper class (R$ 15k+ monthly income)
- **Future:** Low-income (social tariffs, different value proposition)

**By Property Type:**
- **Primary:** Single-family homes
- **Secondary:** Apartments in condominiums
- **Future:** Commercial properties, industrial facilities

**By Education:**
- **Primary:** College-educated professionals
- **Secondary:** High school educated, tech-comfortable
- **Future:** All education levels with enhanced UI/UX

### 4.3 Psychographic Segmentation

**By Motivation:**
- **Cost Savers** - Primarily want to reduce bills
- **Environmentalists** - Primarily want to reduce impact
- **Validators** - Want to ensure they're not overcharged
- **Optimizers** - Want maximum efficiency

**By Tech Adoption:**
- **Early Adopters** - Tech enthusiasts, try new solutions
- **Pragmatists** - Adopt if proven value (primary target)
- **Conservatives** - Need significant education and proof

## Summary

The platform serves **three distinct user types** with different needs:

1. **Clients** - Need simplicity, clarity, actionable insights
2. **Consultants** - Need efficiency, professional tools, scalability
3. **Companies** - Need standardization, collaboration, enterprise features

Success requires:
- ✅ **Client-friendly** interface for non-technical users
- ✅ **Professional** capabilities for consultants
- ✅ **Enterprise-ready** features for companies
- ✅ **Role-based** functionality and permissions
- ✅ **Scalable** architecture to support all user types

The MVP focuses on **Clients** and **individual Consultants** to validate the core value proposition before expanding to enterprise features.
