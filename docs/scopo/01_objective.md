# 01 - Project Objective

## Primary Goal
Develop a comprehensive web platform for detailed analysis, simulation, and recommendation of improvements in electricity bills, integrating official data from ANEEL (Brazilian National Electric Energy Agency) and enabling personalized report generation for different user profiles.

## Business Value
- **For Clients**: Understand their electricity bills, identify saving opportunities, and make informed decisions about energy consumption.
- **For Consultants/Companies**: Manage multiple clients, perform advanced analyses, generate branded professional reports, and provide value-added services.
- **For Administrators**: Maintain system integrity, audit user actions, manage permissions, and ensure data quality.

## Success Metrics
- User satisfaction with bill understanding and recommendations
- Accuracy of calculations compared to actual bills (>98%)
- Reduction in average energy costs for users following recommendations
- Number of reports generated and downloaded
- User retention and engagement rates

## Core Problem Being Solved
Many electricity consumers in Brazil struggle to understand their bills due to:
- Complex tariff structures (TUSD, TE, taxes, flags)
- Lack of transparency in charges breakdown
- Difficulty in identifying saving opportunities
- Absence of personalized recommendations
- No easy way to simulate different consumption scenarios

## Solution Approach
Create an intuitive platform that:
1. Simplifies bill data entry with smart validation
2. Automatically fetches current tariffs from official sources
3. Breaks down all charges with clear explanations
4. Provides actionable recommendations based on consumption patterns
5. Allows simulation of different scenarios
6. Generates professional reports for sharing

## Long-term Vision
- Expand to international markets (multi-country support)
- Integrate with utility company systems for automatic bill import
- Implement machine learning for predictive analysis
- Offer marketplace for energy efficiency products and services
- Build a community of energy-conscious users
